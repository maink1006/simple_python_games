# Flappy Craft

![Flappy Craft Gameplay](assets/game.png)

**Flappy Craft** is a simple Flappy Bird-inspired game with a Minecraft-style design.

Control a chicken and fly through obstacles without hitting them. Try to get the highest score and beat your personal best!

## How to Play

Press `SPACE` or click to start the game.
Press `SPACE` or click to make the chicken fly.
Avoid hitting obstacles.
Use the `LEFT` and `RIGHT` arrow keys to choose your animal.

Have fun playing!
